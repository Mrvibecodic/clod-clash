Clod Clash connects your computer to a VPN service using a **subscription link**. The link comes
from your service (provider) — in your account dashboard, a bot or an email. From it the app
downloads the servers, rules, remaining traffic and expiry date on its own; you don't need to enter
anything by hand.

Screenshots show the Russian interface; the English one has the same layout.

<Shot src="ru/home-connected.png" alt="Home screen in simple mode: connected" />

## Four steps

<Steps>
<Step>

**Install the app** for your system from the [download page](/en/download). Details are in
[Installation](/en/docs/user/install).

</Step>
<Step>

**Add a subscription.** On first launch the home screen shows the "Add your subscription" field.
Paste the link and press **Add**. Details are in [Adding a subscription](/en/docs/user/subscription).

</Step>
<Step>

**Connect.** Press the big button. It turns green, "Connected" appears below it, and a session timer
starts running on the button itself.

</Step>
<Step>

**Pick a server.** Tap the server card under the button and choose a server with good latency —
see [Servers](/en/docs/user/servers).

</Step>
</Steps>

By default the button turns on the **system proxy** — browsers and most programs go through it. If
you need absolutely every program to go through the VPN (games, messengers, the console), turn on
**TUN mode** — see [Connection](/en/docs/user/connection).

## What it can do

- **One screen, one button.** The plan name, remaining traffic, expiry date and support links arrive
  together with the subscription and are shown on the home screen.
- **Two connection methods:** the system proxy (for browsers and most programs) and TUN mode, which
  captures the traffic of every app.
- **Server selection** with latency and flags, favorites, automatic choice of the fastest one.
- **The subscription updates itself** — on a schedule, even when the window is closed; if the
  provider's domain can't be reached directly, the download goes through the tunnel that is already
  up.
- **Honest states.** An expired subscription, used-up traffic or an empty panel response are
  explained in words, not with "0 B" and an error.
- **App auto-update** and a separate core update.
- **Support report** in one click — already stripped of subscription addresses and tokens.

The app is a fork of [Clash Verge Rev](https://github.com/clash-verge-rev/clash-verge-rev). Simple
mode is enough for everyday use; rules, connections, logs and config editors are all still there —
in advanced mode and in the advanced settings.
