Once a subscription is added, the app is ready to go. Still, a few settings are worth checking
right away.

## What the connect button turns on

The connect button turns on whatever is checked in **Settings → System Setting** (in advanced
mode — in "Quick actions" on the home screen):

- **System Proxy** — the default. Browsers and most programs go through it. Administrator rights
  are not needed.
- **TUN Mode** — captures the traffic of all programs, including those that ignore the system
  proxy (games, some messengers, the console). Requires the background service.

You can turn on both. The difference is explained in detail in [Connection](/en/docs/user/connection).

If the provider has fixed the connection method, Quick actions show "… — the connection method is set
by your provider", and in the settings the remaining switch says "Set by your provider".

## Starting with the computer

Three switches in **Settings → System Setting**, all off by default:

- **Connect on launch** — turn the connection on as soon as the app has started;
- **Start with the system** — launch the app when you sign in to the system;
- **Start in the tray** — don't show the window on launch, only the tray icon.

Together they give you "turn on the computer — the VPN is already running in the tray".

## Simple or advanced mode

The window is compact by default: the button, the server, traffic and expiry, the provider's links.
The **Advanced mode →** link at the bottom of the home screen expands the window to two columns
with quick actions, network info and tool tiles. The same is available at **Settings → General →
Advanced mode**. What's where is covered in [Home screen](/en/docs/user/home).

## Theme and language

**Settings → General**:

- **Language** — Russian, English and 11 more languages;
- **Theme Mode** — Light, Dark or System;
- **Fit window to content** — the window height adjusts itself; if you resize it by hand,
  the app remembers your size.

<Shot src="ru/settings-basic.png" alt="The General section" width={720} />

Colors, font and the provider's styling are in **Advanced settings → Appearance and behaviour →
Theme Setting**.

## Device identification

**Settings → General → Device identification** (on by default). Many providers limit the number of
devices per subscription and count them by this identifier. The identifier is hashed: the machine
ID itself and the computer name are never sent anywhere. If you turn it off, a provider with a
device limit may refuse service — the app will offer to turn identification back on.

## Subscription notifications

Reminders about the end of the subscription term and traffic are on by default. Turn them off in
**Settings → Advanced settings → Appearance and behaviour → Subscription notifications**.
