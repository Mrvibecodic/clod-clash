The project has two places on Telegram, and they serve different purposes.

## 📣 Group — news and releases

**[t.me/+2lmP1yhxpCE3MDcy](https://t.me/+2lmP1yhxpCE3MDcy)**

Version announcements, what changed, known issues and warnings. Read it
so you don't miss an update; there's no discussion there.

## 💬 Chat — help and discussion

**[t.me/+8BJQXYXYLqM4YWYy](https://t.me/+8BJQXYXYLqM4YWYy)**

Questions about the client, troubleshooting, ideas and wishes. Before you write,
go through the [Common errors](/en/docs/user/errors) section — half of all
questions are answered there.

To get a quick answer, attach to your message:

- the app version and your system (Windows, macOS, Linux);
- what exactly you did and what you got;
- the [support report](/en/docs/user/support) — it's already free of subscription addresses and tokens.

## Where not to go with what

- **The subscription doesn't work, traffic ran out, questions about your plan and payment** — these
  go to your provider, not to us: the project has no access to panels,
  servers or your data.
- **A bug in the app or a code suggestion** — better open an
  [issue on GitHub](https://github.com/Mrvibecodic/clod-clash/issues): there
  the message won't get lost in the feed.
