Clod Clash is an app for Windows, macOS and Linux that connects your computer to a VPN service using
a subscription link. Paste the link, press one button, and your traffic goes through your
provider's servers.

Screenshots show the Russian interface; the English one has the same layout.

The documentation is split into two parts — pick yours.
